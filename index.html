Travelling

One of the things that I like to do is travel. Some of my favorite places to visit have been Paris, Tokyo, and London. Throughout this website, there is travel information, a map that lets you explore the world, images from my travels, and a contact me page if there are any questions.

The thing I most enjoy about travelling is learning about different groups, cultures, and traditions, that I otherwise wouldn't be able to see. Loking at pictures or reading about places are interesting, but the immersive experience of travelling is what I find to be the most interesting.
